## What you get
An open, no-login interface for AI assistants to run ocean-acoustics calculations
and look up vessel data served by Clairwave. Results are provided "as is" for
information, research and planning. They are model estimates built on climatology
and public data and are not a substitute for measurements, certified survey
products or navigational publications. Do not rely on them for safety-of-life,
navigation or regulatory compliance decisions.

## Acceptable use
- Use the endpoint through an AI assistant or your own client at a reasonable
  rate. Compute calls share a platform-wide budget; sustained automated load
  that degrades service for others may be throttled or blocked.
- Do not attempt to access other users' data, probe the platform's internal
  services, or circumvent rate limits.
- Vessel positions come from the AIS network and public sources. You may use them
  for analysis and display; do not represent them as real-time authoritative
  tracking.
